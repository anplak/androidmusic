### User story

As a user, I want the app to generate simple smart playlists and provide a smarter shuffle, so I can quickly get enjoyable mixes from my existing library without manual curation.

### Goals & scope (2–3 days)

- **In scope**
  - **Basic usage stats** (building on existing DB):
    - Track play count.
    - Last played timestamp.
    - Optional: completion ratio (did the user usually finish the track).
  - **Autogenerated playlists (read-only)**:
    - “Most Played” — top N tracks by play count.
    - “Recently Played” — tracks ordered by last played time.
    - “Recently Added” — based on MediaStore added date or first-seen timestamp.
  - **Smart shuffle**:
    - Option on Now Playing / playlist screens to start “Smart Shuffle”:
      - Bias toward favorites and high play-count tracks.
      - Avoid immediate repeats.
  - **UI integration**:
    - New section on Playlists/Home tab for “Smart Playlists”.
    - Clear labels that some playlists are auto-generated.
- **Out of scope**
  - Complex ML-based recommendations.
  - Mood/genre classification beyond simple heuristics.

### Functional requirements

- **FR1**: Each track’s play count and last played time are updated when the user listens.
- **FR2**: The app displays at least three automatically generated playlists:
  - Most Played.
  - Recently Played.
  - Recently Added.
- **FR3**: Selecting any smart playlist starts playback and builds a queue from its content.
- **FR4**: Smart shuffle:
  - Avoids playing the same track twice in a very short sequence.
  - Gives higher probability to favorites and high play-count tracks.

### Non-functional requirements

- **NFR1**: Stats updates must be light-weight and not affect playback smoothness.
- **NFR2**: Autogenerated playlists should be computed quickly (e.g., precomputed or cached).

### UX notes

- **Discoverability**:
  - Show smart playlists near user-created playlists but visually distinguish them (e.g., icon or label).
- **Transparency**:
  - Brief description text for each smart playlist (“Based on your listening history”).
- **Controls**:
  - Smart shuffle toggle/button should be easy to access from Now Playing and playlists.

### Testing & validation

- Confirm:
  - Stats update correctly after partial and full track playback.
  - Smart playlists update over time as listening behavior changes.
  - Smart shuffle feels varied and avoids obvious repetitions.

---

### Overall app state after this story

The app now offers **auto-generated playlists and a smarter shuffle mode**, turning the offline library into something more dynamic and Spotify-like without any server-side logic.

### Value added after this story

Users gain **instant, low-effort mixes** (Most Played, Recently Played, etc.) and a shuffle that feels more intelligent, increasing engagement and perceived “smartness” of the app.
